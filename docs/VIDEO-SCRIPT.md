# Demo video script (3 minutes), draft 1

Voiceover by Rashad. About 430 words, which is about 3 minutes at a calm pace. Screen cues are in brackets. Numbers in {curly brackets} get filled in from the final test bench run.

---

**0:00 to 0:25 · The hook**

[SCREEN: Instagram reels scrolling, then freeze]

Have you ever wondered how Instagram gets it this right? It seems to know what you're going through before you've told anyone.

Right now that understanding is used to pick your next reel. What if it worked for you instead?

**0:25 to 0:55 · Who it's for**

[SCREEN: Arjun's orbit. Maya close, Dev drifting to "around"]

This is Arjun. He's fictional, but you know him. His girlfriend Maya is having the hardest week of her year, and he's been holding her up every night until 3 AM.

He's the strong one. Nobody checks on the strong one, and he'd never ask. He's even gone quiet with his own brother.

**0:55 to 1:50 · Muse Circle**

[SCREEN: Day slider moving through the month. Today tab]

Muse Circle lives inside Muse. Arjun picks up to five people. Muse learns his normal from his own side of Instagram: when he usually stops scrolling, what his reels are usually about, how fast he usually replies.

Late on its own means nothing. Muse only notices when he's off *his* normal.

[SCREEN: Journal on day 26]

When he wants to write, Muse already knows "D" is his brother Dev, and that it's been quiet with Dev since Saturday. It helps him find the word for what he feels, one small question at a time. Stretched thin. Left out.

[SCREEN: Lift card queued, then Sam's phone]

And on day 23, without Arjun asking, his best friend Sam gets this:

"Arjun has had a full week. Arjun is all about F1 right now, so plan a race watch together."

That's all Sam sees. No feelings, no reasons, nothing Arjun wrote. Five days later, Sam sends him tickets to the Vegas Grand Prix.

**1:50 to 2:30 · The proof**

[SCREEN: What crosses panel, then Under the hood]

This is a working prototype. Every part runs on Muse Spark through Meta's Model API.

The writer that drafts the lift only ever sees three things: Arjun's name, Sam's name and one interest. It can't leak what it never saw. Every line is still checked twice before it can go.

Permissions, the weekly limit and the safety rules are plain code, so no model can talk its way past them. Crisis words never become a lift. They show 988, and nothing goes to anyone.

We tested it on 40 fictional cases, including a breakup, a hidden biopsy and a layoff. Across three runs, nothing private leaked, and it made the right call 119 times out of 120. Then we wrote 15 new cases it had never seen, and they caught a real leak, which we fixed.

**2:30 to 3:00 · Why Meta**

[SCREEN: The orbit, the messenger face moving to Sam]

Only Meta has Instagram's understanding and the circle that's already there. Muse can't talk to other people's Muse today. This is our proposal for how it could, safely.

Instagram understands you to show you the next reel. Muse Circle understands you so the people who love you can show up.
